      Controller-driven variant of <Slicer> for reusable components.

      Functionally identical to controller.components.Slicer, but accepts the controller as a prop rather than being bound to a single form instance. Use this when you want to author a shared component (a date range picker, a multi-step section, etc.) that works against any form whose state satisfies its required shape.
